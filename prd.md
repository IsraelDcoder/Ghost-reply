# Executive Summary

GhostReply is no longer an AI dating reply application.

GhostReply is repositioned into an AI-powered Client Communication Coach built specifically for freelancers.

The product helps freelancers communicate confidently throughout the client lifecycle—from first inquiry to negotiation, project communication, payment collection, and difficult conversations.

The goal is not to generate replies.

The goal is to help freelancers make better communication decisions that lead to:

* Winning more clients
* Charging higher prices
* Building trust
* Reducing communication anxiety
* Getting paid faster

GhostReply should feel like having an experienced freelance mentor available 24/7.
